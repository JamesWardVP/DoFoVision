Depth of field uses the thin-lens hyperfocal model. Distances to the LED are measured along the optical axis (the focus plane is perpendicular to the lens axis), so a panned camera sees one side of the wall closer than the other. The top view shows the horizontal slice through the lens axis; the side view shows the vertical slice. The moiré check images the LED pitch onto the sensor (including foreshortening on angled or curved panels), compares it with the photosite pitch, and applies the contrast loss from defocus blur. It ignores lens sharpness and any optical low-pass filter, so treat it as a conservative estimate. Surfaces are checked independently: one LED surface hiding another is not modelled. Dragging the near or far limit keeps the focus where it is and picks the aperture that gives it, rounded to the nearest third of a stop.
